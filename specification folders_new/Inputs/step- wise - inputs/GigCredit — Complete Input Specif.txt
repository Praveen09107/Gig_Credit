# GigCredit — Complete Input Specification (Steps 1–9)
**Version:** 2.1 — Production Planning Reference
**Status:** Final — All Steps Locked (Inputs Only)
**Scope:** All user-facing fields, toggles, uploads, and mandatory rules for every onboarding step

***

## Overview

GigCredit is a gig-worker credit scoring platform that collects financial, identity, and behavioural data across 9 onboarding steps. This README is the single reference document for all input fields across all steps.

### System Dimensions

| Dimension | Detail |
|---|---|
| Total Onboarding Steps | 9 |
| Steps with Mandatory Content | Steps 1, 2, 3, 4 (partial), 5 |
| Steps with Conditional Content | Step 4 (rent), Step 7 (vehicle insurance) |
| Steps with Optional-Only Content | Steps 6, 7 (mostly), 8, 9 |
| Dynamic Steps | Step 5 (fully reconfigures by work type) |
| Supported Work Types | Platform Worker, Vendor/Seller, Skilled Tradesperson, Freelancer |

### Input Count Summary

| Step | Section | Mandatory | Optional | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic Profile | 11 | 1 | 12 |
| 2 | Identity / KYC | 6 | 0 | 6 |
| 3 | Bank Verification | 6 | 11 | 17 |
| 4 | Utility Bills | 18 | up to 13 | 31 |
| 5A | Platform Worker | 8 | 2 | 10 |
| 5B | Vendor / Seller | 3 | 4 | 7 |
| 5C | Skilled Tradesperson | 3 | 3 | 6 |
| 5D | Freelancer | 2 | 7 | 9 |
| 6 | Government Schemes | 0 (all conditional) | 10 | 10 |
| 7 | Insurance Coverage | 0–1 (conditional) | 2–3 | 3 |
| 8 | ITR and GST | 0 (all conditional) | up to 12 | 12 |
| 9 | EMI / Loan Behaviour | 1 toggle + 4/entry | 1/entry | up to 26 |

### Global Input Rules

- **Required** — must be provided before step completion.
- **Optional** — can be skipped without blocking the step.
- **Conditional Required** — becomes required only when a specific module or toggle is selected.
- Selecting a module and leaving it incomplete **blocks step completion**.
- All document uploads accept JPG, PNG, or PDF unless otherwise noted.

## Step 9 — EMI and Loan Behaviour

**Purpose:** Capture existing loan and EMI obligations to assess monthly debt repayment burden, regularity, and debt-to-income ratio signals. Feeds the P3 Debt Management scoring pillar. Cross-referenced against Step 3 bank statement debit entries.

**UI:** Top-level toggle "Do you have active EMIs?" If Yes, a repeatable loan entry card appears. Up to 5 loan entries allowed.

### Top-Level Toggle

| # | Field Name | UI Element | Input Format | Mandatory |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Active Loans / EMIs? | Toggle Yes / No | Boolean | Required |

> If No → Step 9 marked complete as "No active loans." No further input needed.

### Loan / EMI Entry Card (Repeatable — up to 5)

Each loan the user declares fills one card. Fields below apply per card:

| # | Field Name | UI Element | Input Format | Mandatory |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Lender Name | Dropdown + free text | Bank / NBFC / MFI / Other | Required per entry |
| 3 | Monthly EMI Amount (₹) | Numeric text | Positive integer in INR | Required per entry |
| 4 | Previous Debit Date | Date picker | DD/MM/YYYY | Required per entry |
| 5 | Latest Debit Date | Date picker | DD/MM/YYYY | Required per entry |
| 6 | Loan Verification Trigger | Button (action) | Triggers bank cross-check | ⬜ Optional per entry |

> **Field 4 (Previous Debit Date)** must be earlier than Field 5 (Latest Debit Date).
> **Field 5 (Latest Debit Date)** must be ≤ today's date.
> **Field 6 (Loan Verification)** cross-references declared EMI against Step 3 bank statement debits. Non-blocking.
