STEP-4 — UTILITY BILLS AND RECURRING PAYMENTS
GigCredit Onboarding Specification
Version: Updated — Rent Bill Added
Status: Locked — Inputs Only (No Verification Logic)

--------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------

Step-4 captures recurring payment bills to establish:
  - Address continuity proof
  - Regular payment behaviour
  - Financial obligation consistency

Supported bill types:
  1. Electricity Bill
  2. Water Bill
  3. Gas / LPG Bill
  4. Mobile / Phone Bill      ← existing
  5. Internet / Broadband Bill
  6. Rent Receipt / Agreement ← NEW ADDITION

All modules are optional globally.
If a module is selected, its fields become required.

--------------------------------------------------
2. HOW THIS STEP IS PRESENTED
--------------------------------------------------

Six bill cards displayed vertically.
Each card shows:
  - Bill type and brief description
  - Score impact hint
  - Toggle: "I have this bill" (Yes / No)
  - When Yes → expand fields and upload

8. MODULE 6 — RENT RECEIPT / AGREEMENT ← NEW
--------------------------------------------------

Real-world mapping:
In India, rent proof exists in two forms:
  a) Rent Receipt — issued by landlord monthly,
     contains landlord name, tenant name, address,
     rent amount, and month.
  b) Rent Agreement (Leave & License) — a legal
     document containing tenant name, landlord
     name, property address, monthly rent amount,
     and agreement period (start/end date).

Both are widely used as address and payment proofs.
These fields exist on real documents, so they are
valid explicit inputs.

Section A — Selection

#  | Field Name               | UI Element       | Input Format   | Mandatory
---|--------------------------|------------------|----------------|----------
32 | Rent Proof Selected?     | Toggle Yes / No  | Boolean        | Optional

Section B — Rent Details
(Visible only when Selected? = Yes)

#  | Field Name               | UI Element          | Input Format               | Mandatory
---|--------------------------|---------------------|----------------------------|----------
33 | Tenant Name              | Single-line text    | Plain text                 | Conditional Required
34 | Landlord Name            | Single-line text    | Plain text                 | Conditional Required
35 | Rental Address           | Multi-line text     | Plain text (full address)  | Conditional Required
36 | Monthly Rent Amount (₹)  | Numeric field       | Positive integer           | Conditional Required
37 | Rent Period / Month      | Month-Year picker   | MM/YYYY                    | Conditional Required
38 | Rent Document Upload     | File upload         | JPG/PNG/PDF                | Conditional Required

Field notes:
- Field 33 (Tenant Name):
    Name of the person paying rent — must match
    Step-1 name for address proof purposes.
- Field 34 (Landlord Name):
    Name of property owner as printed on receipt
    or agreement.
- Field 35 (Rental Address):
    Full address of the rented property. Used for
    address continuity cross-check with Step-1
    current address.
- Field 36 (Monthly Rent Amount):
    Rent in ₹ as stated on the receipt/agreement.
    Cross-checked against bank statement debits
    from Step-3.
- Field 37 (Rent Period / Month):
    Month and year the receipt/agreement covers.
- Field 38 (Rent Document Upload):
    Accepted documents:
      • Monthly rent receipt (signed by landlord)
      • Registered or notarised rent agreement
      • Leave and License agreement PDF
