STEP-7 — INSURANCE COVERAGE
GigCredit Onboarding Specification
Version: Final Input Structure
Status: Locked — Inputs Only (No Verification Logic)

--------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------

Step-7 captures the user’s insurance coverage as
evidence of financial resilience and risk management.

This document defines ONLY user-facing inputs:
  - Selection toggles
  - Text fields
  - File upload fields
  - Mandatory / Optional rules

All OCR, API checks, and validation logic are defined
in a separate Step-7 verification specification.

Supported insurance types:
  1. Health Insurance
  2. Vehicle (Motor) Insurance
  3. Life Insurance

Global rule:
- All three insurance types are OPTIONAL at the step level.
- Vehicle Insurance becomes CONDITIONALLY REQUIRED when
  Step-1 Vehicle Ownership = Yes.

--------------------------------------------------
2. HOW THIS STEP IS PRESENTED
--------------------------------------------------

The screen shows three vertically stacked insurance cards:
  - Health Insurance
  - Vehicle Insurance
  - Life Insurance

Each card contains:
  - A short description of what it covers
  - A score impact hint (UX only, not inputs)
  - A toggle: "I have this insurance" (Yes / No)
  - When Yes → expand: show Policy Number, Policy Holder
    Name, and Policy Document Upload fields.

Dynamic behaviour:
  - Health Insurance → always optional.
  - Life Insurance   → always optional.
  - Vehicle Insurance:
      • Required if Step-1 Vehicle Ownership = Yes.
      • Optional (or hidden) if Vehicle Ownership = No.[file:20][file:33]

--------------------------------------------------
3. MODULE 1 — HEALTH INSURANCE
--------------------------------------------------

Real-world documents:
Indian health insurance policy schedules and bonds
always contain:
  - Policy Number
  - Policy Holder Name
  - Insurer Name
  - Sum Insured
  - Policy Start/Expiry Dates.[file:33][web:63][web:69][web:72][web:75]

These are printed on the policy schedule or certificate,
so “Policy Number” and “Policy Holder Name” are valid
explicit input fields.

Section A — Selection

#  | Field Name                    | UI Element         | Input Format       | Mandatory
---|-------------------------------|--------------------|--------------------|----------
1  | Health Insurance Selected?    | Toggle Yes / No    | Boolean            | Optional

Section B — Policy Details
(Visible only when Selected? = Yes)

#  | Field Name                    | UI Element         | Input Format       | Mandatory
---|-------------------------------|--------------------|--------------------|----------
2  | Health Policy Number          | Single-line text   | Alphanumeric ID    | Conditional Required
3  | Policy Holder Name            | Single-line text   | Plain text         | Conditional Required
4  | Health Policy Document Upload | File upload        | JPG/PNG/PDF        | Conditional Required

Notes:
- Field 2 maps to the Policy Number printed on the
  health policy schedule/bond.[web:63][web:69][web:75]
- Field 3 maps to the insured / proposer name printed
  on the policy schedule.[web:63][web:69][web:75]
- Field 4 accepts:
    • Scanned policy schedule
    • E-policy PDF
    • Insurer-issued certificate

--------------------------------------------------
4. MODULE 2 — VEHICLE INSURANCE
--------------------------------------------------

Real-world documents:
Indian motor insurance certificates and policy
schedules always contain:
  - Policy Number
  - Vehicle Registration Number
  - Policy Holder Name
  - Insurer Name
  - Period of Insurance.[file:33][web:67][web:70][web:76]

These are printed on the car/bike insurance policy
document, so these are valid fields to reference.

Section A — Selection

#  | Field Name                    | UI Element         | Input Format       | Mandatory
---|-------------------------------|--------------------|--------------------|----------
5  | Vehicle Insurance Selected?   | Toggle Yes / No    | Boolean            | Optional at UI level

Dynamic mandatory rule:
  - If Step-1 Vehicle Ownership = Yes:
        → This module must be completed before Step-7 exit.
  - If Step-1 Vehicle Ownership = No:
        → User may leave this module off (never selected).[file:20][file:33]

Section B — Policy Details
(Visible only when Selected? = Yes)

#  | Field Name                    | UI Element         | Input Format       | Mandatory
---|-------------------------------|--------------------|--------------------|----------
6  | Vehicle Policy Number         | Single-line text   | Alphanumeric ID    | Conditional Required
7  | Policy Holder Name            | Single-line text   | Plain text         | Conditional Required
8  | Vehicle Insurance Document    | File upload        | JPG/PNG/PDF        | Conditional Required

Notes:
- Field 6 maps to the policy number printed on the
  motor insurance certificate/policy schedule.[web:67][web:70][web:76]
- Field 7 maps to the insured’s name as printed on
  the policy (can be owner or another family member).[web:67][web:76]
- Field 8 accepts:
    • Motor insurance certificate (third party / comprehensive)
    • Policy schedule PDF from insurer portal

Indian life insurance policy bonds and premium
certificates always contain:
  - Policy Number
  - Policy Holder / Life Assured Name
  - Sum Assured
  - Premium Amount
  - Policy Term.[file:33][web:65][web:68][web:71][web:74][web:77]

These are printed on the policy bond or certificate,
so “Policy Number” and “Policy Holder Name” are valid
explicit input fields.

Section A — Selection

#  | Field Name                    | UI Element         | Input Format       | Mandatory
---|-------------------------------|--------------------|--------------------|----------
9  | Life Insurance Selected?      | Toggle Yes / No    | Boolean            | Optional

Section B — Policy Details
(Visible only when Selected? = Yes)

#  | Field Name                    | UI Element         | Input Format       | Mandatory
---|-------------------------------|--------------------|--------------------|----------
10 | Life Policy Number            | Single-line text   | Alphanumeric ID    | Conditional Required
11 | Policy Holder Name            | Single-line text   | Plain text         | Conditional Required
12 | Life Policy Document Upload   | File upload        | JPG/PNG/PDF        | Conditional Required

Notes:
- Field 10 maps to the policy number printed on the
  life policy bond/certificate.[web:71][web:74]
- Field 11 maps to the Life Assured / policyholder
  name printed on the bond.[web:71][web:74]
- Field 12 accepts:
    • Policy bond scan
    • Premium paid certificate PDF
    • Insurer e-policy PDF

--------------------------------------------------
6. STEP-7 INPUT SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------

Module           | Selection Field | Text Fields per Module             | Upload Fields per Module | Mandatory Behaviour
-----------------|-----------------|------------------------------------|--------------------------|---------------------
Health Insurance | 1               | 2 (Policy Number, Holder Name)     | 1 (Policy Document)      | All Conditional Required when selected
Vehicle Insurance| 5               | 2 (Policy Number, Holder Name)     | 1 (Policy Document)      | Conditional Required when selected; overall module required if Step-1 has_vehicle = true
Life Insurance   | 9               | 2 (Policy Number, Holder Name)     | 1 (Policy Document)      | All Conditional Required when selected

Global rules:
- User may select any combination of Health, Vehicle,
  and Life Insurance.
- For each insurance type with Selected? = Yes:
    → Policy Number, Policy Holder Name, and Policy
      Document Upload are REQUIRED before Step-7
      can be marked complete for that type.[file:36]
- Step-7 itself is part of the onboarding flow, but
  only Vehicle Insurance can become structurally
  mandatory based on Step-1 Vehicle Ownership.[file:20][file:33]
